## Decision and scope

Chuck Russell approved the described issue 3 changes with "Approve - make those
changes" on 2026-10-27.

Align the phase-planning skill and phase-loop guide on advance drafting versus
formal acceptance. The project-wide phase plan describes the full phase
structure, requirement ownership, dependencies, coverage contract, and
integration criteria. Phase build plans may be drafted ahead to clarify scope
and non-goals. Detailed tactical plans and construction directives are developed
and finalized just before their phase, informed by prior-phase learnings.

## Acceptance boundary

Advance drafts remain Draft. They do not enter, close, or advance a checkpoint,
issue a future-phase build prompt, authorize code generation, or establish that
future work has been executed or accepted.

Formal acceptance still requires the applicable checkpoint prerequisites,
required approval, and pinned upstream authority. The phase sequence, G5.0 and
G5.<id>.1 through G5.<id>.4 checkpoint definitions, phase-exit conditions, and
approval requirements retain their existing meaning. The normative gate
definitions remain unchanged. Phase N+1's tactical plan cites phase N's
learnings; the first phase records N/A.
